# Aqualogic social icons

Square avatars sized for LinkedIn company profiles (400×400 px) and other social profile pictures.

Two approved variants:

- `aqualogic-linkedin-avatar-on-ink.png` — white mark on Ink background. The reversed treatment, used as the default LinkedIn avatar.
- `aqualogic-linkedin-avatar-on-white.png` — Aqua Cyan mark on white background. Use where a lighter avatar is preferred.

Both are 400×400 PNG, transparent-safe outside the square so they crop cleanly into a circle on platforms that mask avatars.

When new social icons are produced, drop them into this folder and they will appear automatically on `/aqualogic/downloads` and `/linkedin`.
